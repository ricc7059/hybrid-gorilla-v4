# Rotation Table

Exercise selection for the 8-week block. Original programming, written to the method abstracted
in [`METHOD.md`](METHOD.md). Scenario-independent — deficit and maintenance change *round counts*,
not *selection*.

---

## 1. How the block rotates

Seven lift slots, one per day. Each slot carries **four variants**, cycled twice across the block:

| Week | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Variant** | V1 | V2 | V3 | V4 | V1 | V2 | V3 | V4 |
| **Intensifiers** | — | — | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| | | | | | | | | **deload** |

Every variant is seen twice, at weeks *N* and *N+4*. **The second exposure is the progression
target: beat the first exposure's top set.** Four variants at two exposures beats eight one-shot
variants, which would leave nothing to progress against.

Week 8 is the mandatory deload — V4 at roughly half the rounds, effort capped at Medium Plus, no
intensifiers, no finishers taken to failure. The 3-mile time trial lands on that Saturday.

**Constant across all four variants of a slot:** the target muscle group, the movement pattern,
and the supporting cast. **Rotating:** implement, angle, unilateral/bilateral, and the wave shape
carried by the primary. Novelty is concentrated in the primary; the accessories are deliberately
boring.

## 2. The week

| Day | Run | Lift slot | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | Easy | Heavy Upper — horizontal push + vertical pull | Wave loaded, ~40 min |
| **Tue** | Quality | Pump — delts | Isolation to failure + finisher, ~25 min |
| **Wed** | Easy | Heavy Lower — the only hard leg day | Wave loaded, ~45 min |
| **Thu** | Quality | Pump — arms | Isolation to failure + finisher, ~25 min |
| **Fri** | Easy | Vertical push + horizontal pull | Moderate, ~35 min |
| **Sat** | **Long run** | Chest + arms — **zero legs** | Slack day, ~45 min |
| **Sun** | Easy | Back + rear delts | Slack day, ~40 min |

**Weekly exposures:** chest 2 · back 3 · delts 3 · arms 2 · legs 1 heavy (+ 7 days running).

Chest moving from *one* session to *two* is the fix for the ~5-hard-sets-all-Monday problem.
Delts stay at 3 to preserve the X-frame priority carried from 2.0.

## 3. Monday — Heavy Upper

`TARGET: Chest, Lats, Triceps`

| | Primary A — horizontal press | Primary B — vertical pull | Wave shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| **V1** | Barbell Bench Press | Weighted Pull-Up (pronated) | Classic |
| **V2** | Incline Dumbbell Press (30–40°) | Weighted Chin-Up (supinated) | Ramp |
| **V3** | Flat DB Press — single/single/double | Neutral-Grip Pull-Up | Pyramid |
| **V4** | Weighted Dip | Close-Grip Lat Pulldown | Double Wave |

**Supporting cast** (constant): cable or DB fly 10–15 Medium Plus · face pull 10–15 · plank variant 45–60 s

**Alternates** — Travel Best: barbell → dumbbell; pull-up → lat pulldown.
Travel Worst: press → deficit / feet-elevated push-up or band press; pull → band pulldown or suspension row.

---

## 4. Tuesday — Pump: Delts

`TARGET: Front, lateral, and rear deltoid; traps`

Same exercises as 3.0, same 25 minutes. **The change is that the lateral raise is now waved and
the last set goes to failure** — RIR 3 is gone. This is the direct answer to the pump problem and
it costs no extra time.

| | Lateral (primary, waved) | Rear delt | Front/press touch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **V1** | DB Lateral Raise | Bent DB Rear Delt Raise | DB Front Raise |
| **V2** | Single-Arm Cable Lateral Raise | Cable Rope Face Pull | Cable Front Raise |
| **V3** | DB Lateral Raise with Top Hold | Suspension Rear Delt Fly | Plate/DB Overhead Hold |
| **V4** | Band Lateral Raise + **built-in drop-set series** | Band Pull-Apart, high volume | Band Front Raise |

**The lateral wave:** 25 Medium → 20 Medium Plus → 15 Heavy → 10 As Heavy as Possible.
Four rounds, four minutes, and a completely different stimulus from three flat sets.

**V4 drop-set series** — one *set* is the whole chain: 10 reps, drop, 10, drop, 10, to a floor
weight. Costs ~3 minutes.

**Finisher** (2–4 rounds): battle rope jumping jacks, shoulder-raise focus.
*Travel Worst:* very light DB continuous raises.

---

## 5. Wednesday — Heavy Lower

`TARGET: Quads, hamstrings, glutes, calves`

The only hard leg session in the week, and the longest. Lands before an easy run.

| | Primary A — squat | Primary B — hinge | Wave shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| **V1** | Safety Bar Squat | Conventional Deadlift | Pyramid |
| **V2** | Barbell Front Squat | Romanian Deadlift | Classic |
| **V3** | KB/DB Goblet Squat | Single-Leg KB RDL | Autoregulated Ascent |
| **V4** | Bulgarian Split Squat | **Kettlebell Swing** | Ramp |

**Supporting cast** (constant): Nordic curl · walking or reverse lunge 16–20 · standing calf raise 20–30 · wall sit 60 s

The V4 kettlebell swing carries the explosive/triple-extension role the slam ball would have
had. Note that only one hinge variant per cycle is a true heavy pull — V1 conventional. V2–V4
descend in systemic cost deliberately, because this session sits 24 h ahead of a quality run.

**Alternates** — Travel Best: safety bar → back squat or hack squat; Nordic → lying leg curl.
Travel Worst: goblet → bodyweight squat with band; RDL → single-leg bodyweight RDL; Nordic → slider or band leg curl.

## 6. Thursday — Pump: Arms

`TARGET: Biceps, triceps`

| | Biceps | Triceps | Structure |
|---|---|---|---|
| **V1** | Alternating DB Curl | Triceps Rope Pressdown | Both waved 20/15/12/10 |
| **V2** | Bottom-Half Alternating Curl with Hold | DB Skullcrusher | Constant reps, ascending effort |
| **V3** | Cable Curl with High Hold | Reverse-Grip Single-Arm Cable Extension | Ramp |
| **V4** | Double DB Curl + **drop-set series** | Overhead Rope Extension + **drop-set series** | Terminal drop chains |

**Finisher — the ladder.** Cable pulldown → triceps extension, ascending: 1 of each, then 2 of
each, up to 5 of each. On the second exposure (weeks 5–7) run it **descending**: 5 of each down
to 1. Large stimulus per unit time, and mentally the opposite of a flat set.

## 7. Friday — Vertical Push + Horizontal Pull

`TARGET: Shoulders, upper back, traps`

| | Primary A — vertical press | Primary B — horizontal pull | Wave shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| **V1** | Strict Overhead Barbell Press | Barbell Bent-Over Row | Double Wave |
| **V2** | Standing Alternating DB Press | Close-Grip Seated Cable Row | Classic |
| **V3** | KB Strict Clean to Overhead Press | Single-Arm DB Row | Ramp |
| **V4** | Seated Neutral-Grip DB Press | Chest-Supported or Suspension Row | Pyramid |

**Supporting cast** (constant): DB shrug 15–20 Medium Plus · high bent-elbow external rotation 10 Light/Medium · plank shoulder taps 20

The V3 clean-to-press is the one explosive press in the week — it also serves as the ladder
vehicle if you want a second ladder exposure.

---

## 8. Saturday — Chest + Arms · **zero legs**

`TARGET: Chest, triceps, biceps`

Rebuilt from 3.0's "optional upper pump" into a real session. Slack day — absorbs volume. Follows
the long run, so **nothing here loads the legs**: no squats, no lunges, no wall sits, no standing
calf work.

| | Chest primary (angle rotation) | Chest secondary |
|---|---|---|
| **V1** | Incline DB Press | Incline DB Fly |
| **V2** | Flat DB Press | Mid Cable Crossover |
| **V3** | Standing Cable Fly, crucifix position | Push-Up, controlled to failure |
| **V4** | Dip or Equalizer Dip | Low-to-High Cable Fly |

**Arms giant set** (constant, 3–4 rounds): curl variant · close-grip push-up or squeeze press · triceps extension variant

**Finisher** (2–4 rounds): battle rope chest fly → push-up flow. 10 s rope fly, 5 push-ups, repeat
to 60 s. This replaces the slam-ball metabolic role.
*Travel Worst:* rope fly → plank-to-push-up flow, same timing.

> **No burpees here**, and no jump work — a burpee is a squat-and-jump, which would violate the
> zero-legs rule on the day it matters most. This is exactly the kind of creep the rule exists to
> catch.

---

## 9. Sunday — Back + Rear Delts

`TARGET: Lats, mid-back, rear delts`

Slack day. Where the added weekly volume mostly lands. Deliberately high-rep and rep-total driven
rather than heavy — Monday already owns the heavy vertical pull, so this is the same pattern at a
different rep character, which is exactly how the method varies a slot.

| | Primary — pull volume | Secondary | Structure |
|---|---|---|---|
| **V1** | Pull-Up — **50 rep total**, as many per round as possible | Alternating DB Curl between rounds | Rep-total block |
| **V2** | Lat Pulldown | Straight-Arm Rope Pulldown | Waved 15/12/10/8 |
| **V3** | Suspension Row ladder | Parallette or Equalizer Row | Ascending ladder |
| **V4** | Wide-Grip Pulldown | Close-Grip Seated Row | Autoregulated Ascent |

**Supporting cast** (constant): rear delt raise 15 · DB shrug 20 · face pull 15 · dead hang 30–45 s

**Finisher** (2–3 rounds): battle rope anacondas or jump slams, 20–30 s.

## 10. Round counts by scenario

Selection above is fixed. These are the dials.

| Block | Maintenance | Deficit |
|---|---|---|
| Mon / Wed / Fri primaries | 4–6 rounds | 3–5 |
| Tue / Thu isolation | 4 rounds (the wave) | 4 rounds (the wave) |
| Tue / Thu finisher | 3–4 rounds | 2 |
| Sat chest primary | 5–6 | 4 |
| Sat arms giant set | 4 | 3 |
| Sun pull volume | up to 8 | 5 |
| Intensifiers | weeks 3–7 | weeks 5–7 only |

The Tue/Thu isolation wave stays at four rounds in both scenarios — it *is* the pump fix, it's
systemically cheap, and cutting it would reintroduce the exact problem 4.0 exists to solve.

## 11. Open items this creates

- **Nordic curl placement.** Carried over from 2.0/3.0 as a constant on Wednesday. It's the most
  systemically expensive accessory in the week and it sits a day before a quality run. Worth
  watching in weeks 1–2 and moving to Sunday if Thursday's run quality suffers.
- **Battle rope dependency.** The rope now carries three finishers. Travel Best and Travel Worst
  each need a designated alternate for every rope prescription, not just a note.
- **Saturday leg discipline.** The zero-legs rule has to be enforced in the app, not just the
  document — no standing calf raise, no wall sit, nothing that creeps back in via a supporting
  cast copied from another day.
