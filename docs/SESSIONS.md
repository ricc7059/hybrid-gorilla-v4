# Session Templates

The seven daily sessions as concrete lettered blocks with per-round prescriptions.

**Structure is constant; the exercise rotates.** Each day is written once as a template — the
`[bracketed]` slots are filled from the week's variant in [`ROTATION.md`](ROTATION.md). That's the
whole point of the method: novelty lives in the primary movement, not in re-architecting the
session every week.

---

## 1. Wave shape library

Every waved primary carries one of these. The shape rotates by variant, so the same movement
pattern feels different across the block even before the implement changes.

Reps and effort are **per round**. This is the structure the current 3.0 schema cannot express.

### RAMP — reps constant, effort ascends
Best where rep quality matters more than load: pulls, rows, presses you don't want grinding.

| Round | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reps | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 |
| Effort | Medium | Medium Plus | Medium Plus | Heavy | Heavy |

### CLASSIC — reps descend, effort ascends
The workhorse. Ramps to a genuine top set in four rounds.

| Round | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reps | 12 | 10 | 8 | 6 |
| Effort | Medium | Medium Plus | Heavy | As Heavy as Possible |

### PYRAMID — ascend to a peak, then back off
Peak plus back-off volume in one block. The back-off round references an earlier round's load.

| Round | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reps | 12 | 10 | 8 | 5 | 8 | 10 |
| Effort | Medium | Medium Plus | Heavy | **AHAP** | Heavy | *same weight as R2* |

### DOUBLE WAVE — two peaks with a deload between
Highest skill demand. The second peak usually beats the first — that's the point.

| Round | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reps | 12 | 10 | 6 | 10 | 8 | 5 |
| Effort | Medium | Medium Plus | **Heavy** | Medium Plus | Heavy | **AHAP** |

### AUTOREGULATED ASCENT — no prescribed loads
Fixed reps. Add weight every round until you can no longer reach the rep target; stop there.
Self-calibrates across gym modes, which makes it the right shape for travel weeks.

| Round | 1 → N |
|---|---|
| Reps | 12 each round |
| Effort | Add load each round until 12 is unreachable. Typically 4–6 rounds. |

### ISOLATION WAVE — high reps descending, terminal failure
Tuesday and Thursday only. Four rounds, four minutes, and the single most important change in 4.0.

| Round | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reps | 25 | 20 | 15 | 10 |
| Effort | Medium | Medium Plus | Heavy | **As Heavy as Possible → last set To Failure** |

*Arms use a compressed version: 20 / 15 / 12 / 10.*

## 8. Sunday — Back + Rear Delts · ~40 min

Slack day. Where most of the added weekly volume lands. Deliberately high-rep and rep-total

`A` **SPECIFIC WARMUP** — 2 rounds · rest 60 s
| | Exercise | Reps | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Band pulldown | 20 | Light |
| 2 | Dead hang | 20 s | Bodyweight |

| Variant | Structure | Prescription |
|---|---|---|

## 9. Intensifiers — weeks 3–7, isolation only

Applied to the **final round only** of an isolation block. Never on a compound — compound-to-
failure is systemically expensive and the running pays for it the next morning.

| Weeks | Intensifier | How |
|---|---|---|
| **3, 5, 7** | **Drop set** | On the last round, complete the prescription, drop 5–10 lb, continue to failure, drop again. Three drops total. |
| **4, 6** | **Rest-pause** | On the last round, take it to failure, rest 15 s, rep out again, rest 15 s, rep out a third time. |

**Eligible blocks:** Tuesday `B` (lateral) · Thursday `B` (both arms) · Saturday `C` (arms giant set).
**Never:** Monday `B`, Wednesday `B`/`C`, Friday `B`.

Under the **deficit** scenario, run intensifiers in **weeks 5–7 only** — in a deficit they buy
fatigue faster than they buy growth.

## 10. Deload — week 8

Mandatory. Not optional, not "if you feel like it."

| | Deload rule |
|---|---|
| Rounds | Cut to ~50%, rounded up |
| Effort ceiling | **Medium Plus.** No Heavy, no AHAP, no To Failure anywhere |
| Intensifiers | None |
| Finishers | 1–2 rounds, not to failure |
| Wave shapes | Truncate — run rounds 1–3 of the shape and stop |
| Saturday | **3-mile time trial** replaces the long run. Lift is warmup block + block `B` at 3 rounds only |
| Nutrition | **Do not cut calories.** This is when deferred recovery gets taken |

Recalibrate VDOT zones from the TT result before week 1 of the next block.

## 11. What this tells the data model

Writing the sessions out surfaces the schema requirements concretely. For step 5:

- **A round is the atomic unit** and carries: reps, effort label, and optionally its own movement,
  an effort *condition* ("last set to failure"), and a load back-reference ("same weight as R2").
- **Rounds must be individually checkable** — a six-round pyramid is not done until round 6, and
  mid-session you need to know which round you're on and what it asks for.
- **Blocks carry type, round count, rest interval, and a `TARGET` string** independent of their
  exercises.
- **Exercise slots are references, not literals** — `[Primary A]` resolves against the week's
  variant, so one template renders 4 different sessions.
- **Alternates are a field on the exercise**, resolved by gym mode, not a footnote in the text.
- **Round counts are scenario-scaled** — the same template renders at deficit or maintenance
  settings (see [`ROTATION.md`](ROTATION.md) §10).
- **Week number drives three things**: variant selection, intensifier type, and deload state.

## 12. Known tensions

- **Wednesday sits 24 h ahead of Thursday's quality run.** Inherent to keeping heavy lower on
  Wednesday, which the brief requires. Mitigated by descending the hinge cost across variants,
  but worth watching in weeks 1–2.
- **Nordic curl** is the most expensive accessory in the week and it's on Wednesday. First thing
  to move to Sunday if Thursday's run quality degrades.
- **Battle rope carries four finishers.** Travel Best and Travel Worst need a real designated
  alternate for each, not a note.
- **Time budget is honest but untested.** The estimates above assume the prescribed rest is
  actually taken. If Wednesday routinely runs past 50 minutes, cut block `D` to 2 rounds before
  cutting anything from `B` or `C`.
